Meet your dentist

C.D. E.E. Karina González Robles

Dental Surgeon · Endodontics specialist · 14 years in practice

I studied at the Universidad Autónoma de Baja California and I have spent 14 years caring for my patients’ oral health. I hold a specialty in Endodontics and diplomas in dental aesthetics, because my commitment is to keep learning so I can give my patients the quality of care they deserve.

Since I was small I wanted to do something that would let me help others. It is a beautiful thing to see that through my work I can give people back their confidence, their self-esteem, and the smile they had lost, and take their pain away. The extra part is that we have fun along the way. I always try to make the dentist a pleasant experience.
