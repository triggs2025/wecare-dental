Step by step

Directions start the moment you clear the Mexican side of the crossing.

  1. Cross into Mexico

    Come through the San Luis port of entry. Straight ahead you are on Calle 1ra for about one block.

  2. Turn left onto Avenida Álvaro Obregón

    This is the main avenue through town. It is the first major left after the crossing.

  3. Stay on Obregón for about 1.1 miles

    Keep going straight past the numbered cross streets. You are heading for the block between Calle 14 and Calle 15.

  4. Look for the teal front, next to La Cochinita

    The office is at Avenida Álvaro Obregón 1407, on your left, between Calle 14 and Calle 15. Look for the teal storefront with the WE CARE logo, right beside La Cochinita restaurant. Call or message us on WhatsApp if you go past it.
